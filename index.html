A California Original

More Than a Restaurant

Established in 1954, Harry's has spent generations serving quality food in generous quantities at fair prices. The Carvery, hand-carved meats, changing daily specials, full bar and people behind the counter are all part of the tradition.

Harry's uses more than one million pounds of natural whole-bodied Tom turkeys a year. Alongside turkey, our carvers serve roast beef, smoked ham, custom-cured corned beef, New York-style pastrami, center-cut pork loin and rotisserie chicken.
